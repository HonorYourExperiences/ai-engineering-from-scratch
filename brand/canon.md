# WonderCards Canon

WonderCards help young builders, dreamers, and Cape-Able Heroes imagine practical futures through playful, fact-rich cards.

## Worlds
- **Build Your Dreaming Things**: invention, design, and prototyping.
- **Cape-Able Heroes**: strengths, identity, and everyday hero actions.
- **WonderCards**: collectible learning cards for careers, creatures, inventions, influences, and identities.

## Source of truth
Each card starts as structured JSON and is rendered through editable React/TypeScript components. Exported PNG/PDF files are distribution formats, not masters.
